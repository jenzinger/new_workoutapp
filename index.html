Full-Body Dumbbell Routine

7-Exercise Guided Plan with Form Animations

1. Standing Bicep Curl with Supination
Target: Biceps Brachii & Forearms
  • Stand upright holding dumbbells at your sides with palms facing each other.
  • Curl the weights upward while rotating your wrists so palms face up at the top contraction.
  • Keep elbows pinned close to your torso throughout the motion.
2. Dumbbell Bent-Over Row
Target: Latissimus Dorsi, Rhomboids & Rear Delts
  • Hinge at your hips with a flat back and knees slightly bent.
  • Pull dumbbells straight up toward your lower ribcage, leading with your elbows.
  • Squeeze your shoulder blades together at the top of the pull.
3. Dumbbell Chest Press
Target: Pectoralis Major, Front Delts & Triceps
  • Lie flat on your back on the mat or bench with feet planted.
  • Hold dumbbells at chest height with elbows angled at roughly 45 degrees.
  • Press weights straight upward until arms are extended, contracting the chest.
4. Dumbbell Overhead Shoulder Press
Target: Anterior & Lateral Deltoids
  • Stand or sit with core braced and dumbbells positioned at shoulder height.
  • Press the weights vertically overhead without arching your lower back.
  • Lower back down under control until elbows reach 90 degrees.
5. Lying Dumbbell Skull Crusher
Target: Triceps Brachii
  • Lie flat holding dumbbells straight up over your chest with palms facing inward.
  • Flex at the elbows to lower the weights down beside your ears while upper arms stay fixed.
  • Extend your elbows to press the weights back to starting position.
6. Dumbbell Glute Bridge
Target: Gluteus Maximus & Hamstrings
  • Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat on the floor, holding a weight across hips.
  • Drive through your heels to extend your hips upward until shoulders, hips, and knees align.
  • Pause and squeeze glutes at the top before lowering slowly.
7. Abdominal Crunch
Target: Rectus Abdominis
  • Lie back with knees bent and feet flat on the floor.
  • Place hands gently behind head and lift shoulders off the floor using your abdominal muscles.
  • Lower back down smoothly without pulling on your neck.
